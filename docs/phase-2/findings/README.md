The register contains sixty-four validated findings: thirty-nine high, twenty-three medium, and two low. All thirty-nine High conditions are production blockers against AvenChart's adopted future-production quality target; `P2-D014` made FHIR and external laboratory ingestion required, and `P2-D016` resolved the lifecycle and critical-result operating boundaries. They do not imply demonstrated harm, unauthorized mutation, breach, incorrect financial posting, or legal violation in the synthetic Phase 1 experiment. Production-blocker status remains unknown for the Medium findings `P2-03-F014`, `P2-03-F028`, `P2-03-F029`, `P2-04-F001`, `P2-04-F002`, `P2-07-F003`, `P2-07-F004`, `P2-08-F001`, `P2-08-F004`, `P2-08-F005`, `P2-08-F006`, and `P2-10-F001` pending detailed specialist or runtime evidence. No Phase 3 implementation recommendation has yet been accepted, and all gates remain open.
