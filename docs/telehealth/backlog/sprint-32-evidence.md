## 3. Proven boundaries

- only a current `SyntheticAllergyInformationRecorded` applicant with exact promotion, patient, registration, insurance, communication, device, inventory, medication, allergy, safety-location, callback, practice, and facility provenance can read or record the receipt;
- only server-owned topic keys/categories, the additional-or-unlisted signal, four mandatory acknowledgments, and explicit false consequence facts cross the route;
- the local catalog remains incomplete and unmapped, and no topic is represented as a diagnosis, clinical finding, canonical resource, verified/negative assertion, reconciliation, risk modifier, or triage decision;
- a successful command produces one parent, the exact bounded child set, one event, and one monotonic state transition, while any bounded contention conflict is recoverable by unchanged retry; and
- applicant source, canonical clinical, alert/task, patient, insurance, financial, request, queue, appointment, encounter, care, prescribing, claim, integration, and external-action records remain unchanged.

## 4. Evidence-gate observations

The first isolated live attempt reached the older clinical-inventory contention rehearsal and observed seven identical 200 results plus one bounded optimistic-concurrency 409. Later medication and allergy proofs already allowed this fail-closed behavior. The older proof was aligned to require all simultaneous results be 200 or 409, at least one success, exactly one receipt/event, and a subsequent unchanged retry. The restarted chain then passed through the new health-history boundary; all eight concurrent health-history attempts returned the same immutable result.

The first migration invocation deliberately reused the prior packaged image and stopped at V0308, proving that source-file presence alone is insufficient. Rebuilding the image packaged 265 migrations and applied V0309. The full rehearsal later independently bootstrapped and recovered the complete V0309 schema and rejected checksum and unexpected-ledger drift.

## 5. Rollback and stop evidence

- base and Development configuration remain disabled, and Production enablement remains startup-rejected;
- missing, stale, expired, cross-applicant, portal-enabled, merged, patient-drifted, canonical-insurance/medication/prescription/allergy/problem, or provenance-incomplete state cannot record health-history topics;
- immutable parent/child evidence is not destructively rolled back; correction requires a separately reviewed forward migration and workflow;
- disabling or removing the routes and panel leaves the synthetic patient shell portal-disabled and without a canonical clinical finding, verified no-history assertion, reconciliation, risk/triage decision, task, intake completion, eligibility, request, queue, care, or prescribing capability; and
- stop conditions include clinical detail or free text crossing the route; any external coding or completeness claim; a topic becoming diagnostic, verified, reconciled, risk-evaluated, triage-changing, or actionable; routing creating authority; canonical/downstream mutation; provenance divergence; history overwrite; or an earlier safeguard regression.
