# Phase 2 assessment coverage matrix

This is the governed repository inventory and assessment tracker. `Inventoried` means the surface has been located; it does not mean that it has been assessed. Rows advance only when linked evidence supports the stated status, and broad rows remain open until their applicable questions, unknowns, and specialist decisions are reconciled.

## Status vocabulary

- `Inventoried` — located and provisionally scoped
- `Assigned` — reviewer and packet approved
- `In review` — evidence collection has started
- `Evidence complete` — required checks completed; findings may still need validation
- `Validated` — evidence and material findings independently checked
- `Complete` — scorecard, findings, unknowns, and exclusions reconciled
- `Excluded` — excluded by an approved decision with risk and rationale

## Required per-row evidence

When a row advances beyond `Inventoried`, add or link an evidence packet containing:

- reviewer and independent verifier where required;
- baseline commit, review date, tools, and environment;
- applicable domain questions and exclusions;
- entry points, dependencies, data flows, and representative traces;
- commands, results, measurements, screenshots, or query plans;
- strengths, candidate findings, unknowns, and specialist-validation needs;
- scorecard impact and canonical finding IDs after coordinator review.

## Completion controls

- Every domain from 01 through 12 must apply to at least one row and be reconciled across all relevant rows.
- User-visible clinical workflows must be reviewed vertically across UI, API, domain behavior, persistence, audit, and recovery.
- Cross-cutting controls must be reviewed horizontally for consistent enforcement.
- Broad rows must be split when one evidence packet cannot support a credible completion decision.
- Exclusions require a decision-log entry.
