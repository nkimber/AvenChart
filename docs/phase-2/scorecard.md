## Rating rules

- Use the anchored definitions in [quality-standard.md](quality-standard.md).
- Record `low`, `medium`, or `high` confidence independently of the rating.
- A blocker caps the affected domain at `0` until it is disproved, accepted as risk by the program owner, or resolved in a later phase.
- A rating of `3` or `4` requires representative behavioral and operational evidence, not static inspection alone.
- A domain cannot be `Complete` while an applicable coverage row is incomplete or excluded without approval.
- Do not average domain ratings into one number. Phase decisions consider blockers, dependencies, uncertainty, and target use.

Packet 1 supports a provisional `2 — Partial` ceiling for its architecture/structure slices. Packet 2 supports a provisional `2 — Partial` ceiling for data/persistence: mechanism selection is deliberate, but schema-authority and catalog-integrity controls remain unresolved. Packet 3 supports a provisional `2 — Partial` ceiling for quality controls, now strengthened by actual failing/short-circuited deep-gate runs. COV-002 validates five target blockers, COV-003 six after the lifecycle decision, COV-004 ten, COV-005 seven, COV-006 four after the critical-follow-up decision, and COV-007 five. `P2-D014` makes the COV-010 FHIR condition and COV-019 external-laboratory condition two additional blockers. Existing High findings cap Domains 03, 04, 05, 07, and 10 at `0 — Unacceptable` until disproved, accepted by the proper authority, or resolved in a later phase; Domain 08 remains capped pending accessibility and recovery evidence. Domains remain unscored pending qualified specialist evidence, representative performance/concurrency, and current production-topology proof rather than target-policy choices or environment availability alone.
